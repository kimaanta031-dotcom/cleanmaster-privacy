1. Overview

CleanMaster ("the app") helps you free up space on your Android device by clearing temporary files, finding duplicate photos and large videos, listing apps you have not used for a while, and, optionally, moving very old Gmail messages to Trash. This policy explains what the app accesses and how it is used.

In short: the app does not have its own servers, does not collect your personal data, does not show ads, and does not use analytics or advertising SDKs. Your files are analyzed on your device and are never uploaded by the app.

3. How we use the information

The information above is used only to provide the features you request inside the app. We do not use it for advertising, profiling, or any other purpose.

4. Sharing and selling

We do not collect, sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties. Because the app has no servers of its own, we have no copy of your photos, videos, app list, or emails.

5. Gmail and Google user data

The Gmail feature is optional and works only after you sign in with your Google account and grant permission. The app requests the Gmail permission needed to change message labels (so that messages can be moved to Trash).

  • The app reads message identifiers and, to show you a few examples before you approve, the subject and sender of up to five messages. This information is shown on your screen and is not stored or sent anywhere else.
  • Starred, important, sent, and draft messages are excluded.
  • Messages are moved to Trash, where Gmail keeps them for about 30 days before deleting them permanently. Nothing is moved without your explicit approval in the app.
  • CleanMaster's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

You can sign out inside the app at any time, and you can revoke the app's access in your Google Account settings under Security, "Third-party apps with account access".

6. Deleting files and apps

The app never deletes your photos, videos, or apps on its own. It shows you a list, asks for your approval, and then asks Android to perform the deletion. Android may show its own confirmation dialog. Deleted photos and videos may not be recoverable, so please review the list carefully before you confirm.

7. Permissions used

PermissionPurpose
Photos and videos (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, READ_MEDIA_VIDEO; on older Android versions, storage permissions)Finding duplicate photos and large videos, and deleting the ones you select.
Usage access (PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS)Finding apps you have not used recently. Granted by you in system settings.

You can withdraw any permission at any time in your device settings. Some features will stop working without them.

8. Retention and security

The app keeps only your local settings on your device. When you uninstall the app, these settings are removed. Because your files are processed locally and nothing is uploaded by the app, we have no data to retain or delete on our side.

9. Children

CleanMaster is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Third-party services

The app relies on Android system services and, for the optional Gmail feature, on Google Sign-In and the Gmail API. Their handling of data is governed by Google's own privacy policy.
